Color Picker from Image is installed. Three things worth knowing before your first pick.

1 · Pin it

Click the puzzle icon in the toolbar, then the pin next to “Color Picker from Image”. One click from any tab from now on.

2 · Open the picker

Click the icon → “Open picker”. Drop an image, press Ctrl+V, or browse — hover, click, and the HEX is copied.

3 · Pick from pages

“Grab this page” in the popup captures the tab you're on. Or right-click any picture → “Pick color from this image”.
